How to send a payment reminder on WhatsApp to customers (with message templates)

Collecting udhar is the least favourite part of running a shop. You don’t want to sound rude to a customer who has shopped with you for years, but you also can’t let ₹40,000 sit outstanding across 30 people. A short, polite WhatsApp message with a UPI payment link solves most of it.

Five rules for reminders that get paid

  1. Be specific. State the exact amount and the date of the last entry.
  2. Make paying effortless. Include a UPI link or your UPI ID so they can pay from the message.
  3. Pick the right time. 1st–7th of the month (salary days) and weekend mornings work best. Avoid late night.
  4. Stay warm. “Namaste”, their name and “dhanyavaad” go a long way.
  5. Escalate gently. First a friendly note, then a reminder after a week, then a phone call.

How to add a UPI payment link to the message

A UPI link looks like upi://pay?pa=yourname@okbank&pn=Shop%20Name&am=1250&cu=INR. When the customer taps it on their phone, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM opens with the amount pre-filled.

Typing this by hand is error-prone. In DoAide Khata, add your UPI ID once in Settings; then open any customer and tap Remind. WhatsApp opens with the due amount and a ready UPI link in Hindi or English — you just press send.

When a message isn’t enough

If two reminders over two weeks get no response, call. Offer to split the amount (“₹500 aaj, baaki agle hafte”) — partial payment is better than none. Record each part payment as a “Got” entry so the balance stays accurate, and share a PDF statement if they dispute the total.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a polite payment reminder in Hindi?

Greet the customer by name, state the exact amount due and the last entry date, add a UPI link, and thank them. For example: “नमस्ते रमेश जी, आपके खाते में ₹1,250 बाकी हैं। इस लिंक से UPI पर भुगतान कर दीजिए। धन्यवाद।”

Can I send a UPI payment link on WhatsApp?

Yes. A upi://pay link with your UPI ID and amount opens the customer’s UPI app with the amount filled in. DoAide Khata builds this link for you automatically.

How often should I send udhar reminders?

Once at the start of the month, and a follow-up a week later. More often than that tends to annoy regular customers.
