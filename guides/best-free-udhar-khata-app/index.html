Best free udhar khata app for shopkeepers in 2026

Almost every kirana store, medical shop and hardware dealer in India runs on udhar — regular customers take goods today and pay at the end of the week or month. For decades that credit lived in a paper bahi khata. Paper works, but it gets lost, smudged or disputed, and it can’t send a reminder for you.

A digital udhar khata app fixes that. This guide explains what actually matters when you pick one, so you can choose based on your shop rather than on advertising.

Why move your udhar khata from paper to phone

  • Every customer’s balance in one tap. No flipping pages to find what Sharma ji owes.
  • Fewer disputes. Each entry has a date, amount and note. You can share a statement instead of arguing over memory.
  • Faster collection. A polite WhatsApp reminder with a UPI link gets paid much faster than a verbal “bhaiya, hisaab kar dena”.
  • Nothing lost. A backed-up digital khata survives a wet register or a misplaced diary.

Things that matter less than marketing suggests: loans offered inside the app, “credit scores” for your customers, and heavy inventory modules if you only want to track udhar.

Signup, privacy and where your data lives

Many khata apps require your mobile number and OTP before you can make a single entry, and your customers’ names and numbers sit on their servers. Ask three questions before you commit:

  1. Can I use it without creating an account?
  2. Can I export or back up my data myself?
  3. Is it a heavy app download, or does it work in the browser?

DoAide Khata works in your phone’s browser with no signup. Entries are saved on your phone; if you want a backup and sync across phones, you can sign in with Google, or download a backup file from Settings.

How to start in 5 minutes

  1. Open khata.doaide.com and add your top 10 udhar customers with their mobile numbers.
  2. For each, enter the current balance from your paper register as one “Gave” entry with the note “opening balance”.
  3. Add your UPI ID in Settings so reminders carry a payment link.
  4. From tomorrow, record each new udhar and payment as it happens.
  5. At month end, send reminders to everyone with a balance — one tap each.

Keep the paper register for a few weeks in parallel if that gives you confidence, then retire it.
