Looking for a khata book alternative in 2026? Here is what actually matters

Khata apps took off because they solved a real problem: the paper register could not send reminders or add up balances. But after a few years with the popular apps, many shopkeepers find themselves searching for a khata book alternative — usually because of growing ads and promotions inside the app, pressure to take loans, heavy downloads on budget phones, or simply wanting their customer data under their own control.

Why shopkeepers look for an alternative

Across the common khata apps, the complaints shopkeepers raise are remarkably consistent:

  • Clutter. The core job — udhar in, payment in, balance — gets buried under loan offers, insurance banners and promotions.
  • Forced account creation. You cannot even try the app without a mobile number and OTP, and your customers' names and numbers go to someone's server on day one.
  • App weight. Big downloads and background services slow down the budget Android phones most shops actually run on.
  • Lock-in worries. Getting your own data out as a usable file is often hard to find or incomplete.
  • Features you never asked for replacing the simple bahi khata flow you did.

None of this means those apps are bad — they pioneered digital khata. It means your needs (a fast, clean, private ledger) and their business model (lending and cross-selling) may have drifted apart.

Moving your existing khata data across

Switching apps does not mean re-typing history. You only need today's truth:

  1. In your current app, note each customer's current outstanding balance (or export/screenshot the balances screen).
  2. In the new ledger, add each customer and record that balance as one "Gave" entry with the note opening balance.
  3. Add your UPI ID in Settings, send a test reminder to yourself, and check it reads well.
  4. Run the old app read-only for a couple of weeks in case of disputes about older entries, then uninstall it.
